# intro to the system
Weather Research and Forecasting Model
- seems like it is pronounced "wharf"
- distributed development and support
- 25 years old!

Two dynamical cores:
- Advanced Research WRF (ARW)
	- this is probably the one we are going to be looking at; sounds like NMM may be deprecated?
- Nonhydrostatic Mesoscale Model (NMM)

WRF-ARW is a research tool and a tool for numerical weather prediction; can be used for 
- idealized simulations
- experimental real-time forecast
- convection forecast
- high-resolution hurricane simulations
- development of ensemble forecasting technology
- regional climate modeling
- data assimilation research
- chemistry simulation

## How does a numerical model work?
- starts with an atmospheric state, advances in time to look for a solution.
- diffeq form: $\frac{\Delta A}{\Delta t} = F(A)$
	- $\Delta A$ is a change in a forecast variable at a particular point in space
	- $F(A)$ represents the physical and dynamical processes that can change the value of A
	- $\Delta t$ is the change in time
- So a forecast is $A^{forecast} = A^{initial} + F(A)\Delta t$
- Time integration: $A^N = A^{initial} + F_1(A)\Delta t + F_2(A)\Delta t ... + F_N(A)\Delta t$
## How are data represented?
In a numerical model, data are represented with one value per grid cell (discretized)
## Limited area model (LAM)
For a global model (like a map of the world), we only need to know its initial state. But for a limited area (say a small subsection of the world map), we also need to know a bit about what's going on outside of that small area. 
### Lateral Boundary Condition (LBC)
Global data comes into the limited area model at the lateral boundaries. We need this boundary data for the entire simulation. We also need to think about the vertical boundaries (think sea surface, soil layers, etc). Domains are nested, where higher up ones are lower resolution. 

High resolution models produce better results, but they cost a lot more to run. 10 km model (I think referring to grid size) would cost roughly 27x more than a 30km model, 1km costs 1000x more than 10km.
