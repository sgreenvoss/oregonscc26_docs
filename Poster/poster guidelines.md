**Poster Submission Guidelines  
**

- The poster must be in landscape orientation and fit a16:9 ratio monitor.
- The monitor in the SCC Connect booth is 65", so please ensure that your pictures, logos, figures, and fonts are an appropriate resolution and size for display on a screen of this size.
- The poster file must be uploaded as a PDF document.

  
**Poster Guidance  
**  
A good poster should provide a clear and engaging overview of your team's preparation for the competition. Consider highlighting:  

- Your team's background and motivation
- The approach and strategies you took in preparing for the competition
- The technical environment in which you worked, including relevant software, systems, tools, and resources
- The challenges you encountered and how your team addressed them
- Key decisions and tradeoffs you made during your preparation
- How you approached improving, optimizing, or benchmarking your applications
- How your team worked together, including how you organized your time, responsibilities, and training

Please use appropriate figures, visualizations, diagrams, and other supporting materials where helpful to communicate your work and tell a cohesive story.  
  
These topics are intended as **guidance rather than a checklist**. You do not need to address every point explicitly. Instead, use them to help shape a clear, engaging, technical story about your team's experience and preparation for SCC Connect.
